Our Story

Built on a
simple belief —
every child matters.

Founded in 2011, Proper Child International School was born from a conviction that quality education should not be a privilege of geography or wealth. Nestled in Umuotube Chokota, Igbo Etche, we serve families across Rivers State who refuse to settle for less for their children.

Our approach blends academic rigour with character formation — because a diploma without values is just paper. We graduate young people who are not only prepared for examinations, but prepared for life.

Admission is
Open — Let's Talk

We welcome applications for Nursery, Primary, and Secondary classes throughout the year. Our process is simple, honest, and designed with the child's best interest first.

01

Make an Inquiry

Call us, send an email, or simply walk in. We're always happy to answer questions from parents and guardians.

02

Submit Application

Complete the admission form and provide the required documents listed below.

03

Assessment Day

A brief, friendly assessment helps us place your child in the right class for their level and confidence.

04

Welcome to PCIS!

Complete enrollment, collect your child's materials, and join the Proper Child family.

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate or declaration of age
  • Previous school report card (if applicable)
  • 4 recent passport photographs
  • Parent/guardian ID card
